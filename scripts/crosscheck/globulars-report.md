- **SIM**: SIMBAD catalogued V flux
- **BW**: Baumgardt et al. web table v4 (the app's own source; transcription check only)

Baumgardt & Vasiliev 2021 (J/MNRAS/505/5957) is not on VizieR; its distances reach the app only via BW. VizieR VII/202 is the 1997 Harris edition, so the 2010 edition is read from McMaster's mwgc.dat.

## Thresholds

- **d**: MW flagged when >15% from all of H10, BH18 and the Gaia EDR3 parallax, or >15% AND >3 sigma from a precise (plx/err >= 10) parallax; Magellanic/Fornax: >15% from the MC12 host distance
- **mass**: >factor 2 from the geometric median of the independent references (MW: BH18, MV05, Harris M_V at the app distance x M/L 2; LMC: MV05, B13)
- **mag**: >0.4 mag vs H10 V_t (MW) or MV05 / G10 / SIMBAD (Magellanic, Fornax)
- **size**: visual size < (3.5 r_h)/3 or > 3 x (2 r_t) (H10 radii for MW, MV05 King fits otherwise)
- **rhoc**: >0.5 dex from both BH18 and Harris rho_0 x M/L 2; **vesc**: >30% from both BH18 and MV05
- **transcription**: app d, mass (2 s.f.), rhoc, vesc, tdiss vs the Baumgardt web table (BW) the app cites
- **desc_distance**: distance quoted in the catalog description vs the app's own d, >15%

## Other findings

- **Transcription**: all 102 Baumgardt-sourced objects match the BW table exactly (d, mass to 2 s.f.; rhoc, vesc, tdiss). Column mapping verified against the table legend (R_sun col 3, Mass 7, log rho_c 14, T_Diss 18, v_esc 22).
- **Gaia EDR3 parallaxes** (VB21, 56 clusters with plx/err >= 10): no app distance is >3 sigma off (max |sigma| 2.8); median app d / (1/plx) = 1.076, the ~0.007-0.010 mas residual EDR3 parallax offset that BV21 corrected for.
- **V magnitudes (MW)**: the app's mag equals Harris 2010 V_t for 102 of 102 MW globulars. Baumgardt+2020 integrated V (BW) differs by >0.4 mag for 17: MWSC3171 -2.06, M71 +1.03, NGC6355 -0.90, NGC6453 +0.89, NGC6539 -0.61, NGC6316 -0.58, NGC6325 -0.57, NGC6401 -0.52, NGC6642 -0.52, NGC6712 -0.50, NGC6584 -0.49, NGC6101 +0.48, NGC6517 -0.46, NGC5053 -0.45, NGC6540 -0.44, IC1276 +0.42, NGC6558 -0.40 (app - BW). Mostly reddened bulge clusters and sparse/large ones; Harris V_t is the standard observer's total magnitude, so KEEP, but BW masses use BW's V (see Pal 11, NGC 6642).
- **Pal 11 (MWSC3171)**: BW v4 fit rests on Baumgardt+2020 integrated V = 11.86, 2.06 mag fainter than Harris V_t = 9.80 (the app's own mag); BH18 (1.5e5), MV05 and Harris M_V x M/L 2 (1.1e5) all give ~10x more mass. App mag 9.8 + mass 1.4e4 are mutually inconsistent (implied M/L_V ~ 0.25). Same root cause for the low rhoc and vesc. Not a clear error (BW v4 is the newest catalogue) but the merger may prefer consistent values: either keep BW (mass 1.4e4) with V 11.9, or keep V 9.8 with a Harris-based mass ~1e5.
- **Magellanic 'globulars' are mostly young**: NGC1755 25 Myr (Cl*), NGC1786 15 Gyr (Cl*), NGC1818 25 Myr (GlC), NGC1850 32 Myr (Cl*), NGC1854 110 Myr (Cl*), NGC1856 130 Myr (Cl*), NGC1866 130 Myr (GlC), NGC1916 16 Gyr (Cl*), NGC2004 20 Myr (Cl*), NGC2041 25 Myr (Cl*), NGC2100 16 Myr (Cl*), NGC2157 40 Myr (Cl*), NGC2164 50 Myr (OpC), NGC419 no age in B13/MV05 (OpC). Ages from B13 (MV05 where absent); type from SIMBAD. Only NGC 1786 and NGC 1916 are old globulars; NGC 419 (SMC) is ~1.5 Gyr (intermediate age). The rest are young massive clusters (16-130 Myr) that OpenNGC labels GCl. Any copy implying ancient, 12-billion-year-old stars would be wrong for them; type review suggested (not a data correction).
- **Magellanic masses**: app masses exist only for NGC 1818, 1850, 1866, 2004 (Wikipedia) and NGC 419 (not in MV05/B13, unchecked). xcheck JSON carries B13/MV05 masses for the other LMC clusters if the merger wants them.
- **Visual sizes**: none outside the window. Visual size exceeds the Harris tidal diameter 2 r_t for NGC6397 (x1.01), NGC6544 (x1.41); Harris r_t is nominal for core-collapsed clusters, harmless.
- **NGC 1049 mass**: the app has none; MV05 gives 2.5e5 M_sun at 137 kpc (~2.9e5 at 147 kpc), in the xcheck JSON.
- MV05 `vesc0` is labelled log(km/s) on VizieR but the values are linear km/s; used as such.
