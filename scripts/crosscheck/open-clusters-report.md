# Open clusters: cross-check against independent catalogues

Generated by `scripts/crosscheck/open-clusters.py` — re-runnable from `.local/crosscheck-cache/oc-*.csv` (`--offline` uses only the cache). Scope: the 336 catalog objects of type open_cluster, cluster_nebula, asterism and star_cloud; their facts (d, age, members, mass) and catalog visual size. Reference values per object: `shared/data/xcheck/open-clusters.json`; proposed fixes: `open-clusters-corrections.json`.

Sources (open, via VizieR TAP / SIMBAD TAP; no Wikipedia):

- **HR23** — Hunt & Reffert 2023, A&A 673, A114 (Gaia DR3), VizieR J/A+A/673/A114
- **HR24** — Hunt & Reffert 2024, A&A 686, A42 (Gaia DR3 masses), VizieR J/A+A/686/A42
- **CG20** — Cantat-Gaudin et al. 2020, A&A 640, A1 (Gaia DR2), VizieR J/A+A/640/A1

- **MWSC** — Kharchenko et al. 2013, A&A 558, A53 (MWSC, pre-Gaia), VizieR J/A+A/558/A53
- **DAML** — Dias et al. 2002 catalogue v3.5 (pre-Gaia literature compilation), VizieR B/ocl
- **B08** — Bica et al. 2008, MNRAS 389, 678 (Magellanic Cloud objects), VizieR J/MNRAS/389/678
- **SIMBAD** — SIMBAD (identifiers, positions)
- **GaiaDR3** — Gaia DR3, VizieR I/355/gaiadr3

Matching: by name (NGC/IC/Melotte/Collinder/Harvard…, normalised; plus SIMBAD's identifiers for each object, and HR23's own cross-identification lists), checked against position (within the object's diameter or 15′, or 3x the reference's r50). Name matches farther away are reported, not used. Catalogues that split a cluster (NGC 2451A/B) are matched to the nearer part.

Rules: **distance** flagged when >25% from the median of the Gaia catalogues (HR23, CG20, D21); called wrong when those agree with each other (spread ≤25%). **Age** flagged when >2x from the median of the other catalogues *and* those agree (≥2/3 within 2x of their median); called wrong only when every other Gaia-based catalogue is also >2x from ours. **Visual size** flagged when >3x from the median of catalogue core diameters (2·r50 of HR23/CG20/D21, 2·r1 of MWSC, DAML's apparent diameter, B08's major axis); called wrong when it is also <2/3 of the smallest of them. **Members** flagged when the app's count is not the matched HR23 cluster's N, or HR23 and the Gaia DR2 catalogues differ >6x. **Mass** (app value from Wikipedia infoboxes) flagged when >3x from HR24 MassTot; wrong when physically impossible (<0.3 M☉ per Gaia member) or >5x off a mass known to ±30%. **Position** flagged when SIMBAD is farther than max(10′, half the size).
