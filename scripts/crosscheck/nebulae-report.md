## Thresholds

- PN distance: >40% from the Gaia central-star parallax distance when plx/err > 5 (RUWE < 2; Gaia DR3 from C26, else EDR3 from CW21); otherwise > factor 2 from the median of FBP16, SH18, BS23, SH10.
- SNR / emission / reflection / dark distance: > factor 1.6 from the consensus (median of independent anchors: ionising/illuminating star parallax, HR23 cluster, Z20 cloud, LMC, parallax-based literature). A second tier ('precise') also flags a >25% miss when ≥2 anchors agree within 10% (or a ±2% published measurement), i.e. where the threshold would hide a clearly outdated value.
- Angular size: no catalogue within a factor 2 of ours (sizes of diffuse nebulae differ widely between catalogues by definition, so agreement with any one counts).
- Mass: any nebular mass in objectFacts is checked for a published nebular counterpart.

## Notes

- PN central-star parallaxes and our values agree well: most of the app's PN distances already come from Gaia (SIMBAD 'plx'); the failures are the Wikipedia and old-statistical figures.
- M 97 (ours 2,000 ly, Gaia 2,600) and NGC 6543 (3,300 vs 4,300) are 23% short of Gaia — inside the 40% band, but both are Wikipedia figures and Gaia S/N > 20.
- NGC 7027 and NGC 5315 central stars have RUWE > 2.5 (Gaia parallaxes unreliable); checked against statistical distances instead.
- PN sizes: HASH often includes faint halos/lobes, the SEC gives the bright shell, T03 the 10%-of-peak extent. Our values usually follow the SEC, so they pass, but for NGC 2440 (ours 0.27′ vs T03 0.98′, HASH 1.2′), NGC 6302 (0.74′ vs T03 1.5′, HASH 6.5′) and NGC 6445 (0.55′ vs HASH 3.7′) the physical size shown is the bright core only.
- LBN sizes coincide exactly with many of ours (OpenNGC adopted them), so LBN agreement is weak evidence; Sh2, RCW, Cederblad and vdB are independent.
- Masses: only three nebulae carry one; two are stellar masses attached to reflection nebulae.
